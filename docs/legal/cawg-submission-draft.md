## What CAWG is (confirmed — sourced in this repo)

CAWG is found defined elsewhere in this codebase, so this file does **not** need to be a blank placeholder. For the record:

- **CAWG = the Cryptocurrency Advisory Working Group.** This exact name is confirmed as the official name per the Cook Islands Office of the Prime Minister's own website (verified in `cook-islands-establishment-reality-ledger.md`, Part 4, correcting an earlier internal mislabel of "Crypto Assets Working Group").
- **Co-chaired by** the Office of the Prime Minister (OPM) and the **Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)** of the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC).
- **Membership (per OPM's 9 March 2026 announcement):** OPM, FIU, FSC, Crown Law Office, MFEM, Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Immigration, Ministry of Justice.
- **Purpose:** formed to advise the Prime Minister on how to handle crypto policy, specifically in response to a **draft Cryptocurrency (Ransomware Suppression) Bill 2025** that is not yet enacted and has drawn public pushback from the Bankers Association of the Cook Islands and the Cook Islands Chamber of Commerce.
- CAWG **reports internally to the Prime Minister** — per OPM's own release, it is not (as of the last verified check) a body that takes public submissions on an open, standing basis.

This definition is already documented at length in two existing repository files, which this new file deliberately does not duplicate in full:

- `cook-islands-establishment-reality-ledger.md` (repo root) — the underlying research and source citations for all of the above.
