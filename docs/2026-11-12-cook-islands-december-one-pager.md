# IN$DEX: a constructive path while CAWG deliberates

*One-pager for the December 2026 Cook Islands meetings. Draft, 12 November 2026.*

## The problem: silence, and a bill under review

The Cryptocurrency Advisory Working Group (CAWG) was established on 9 March 2026 by the Office of the Prime Minister (OPM) and the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU), who co-chair it. It advises on international and transnational cybercrime, especially ransomware, including AML/CFT compliance, correspondent banking impacts and reputational risk. Preliminary advice was expected "within the coming months". As of November there is no public update.

The Cryptocurrency Ransomware Suppression Bill (2025) remains under review, and an earlier draft was reported to have been set aside on Crown Law advice. That care is warranted. The result is a gap: the Cook Islands has no VASP licensing regime, while Nauru (Command Ridge), the Marshall Islands (USDM1) and Palau (a Ripple stablecoin pilot) have moved. The Cook Islands is behind on licensing, but ahead on trust structuring.

## The precedent: Smart Trust

Under the SMART Economy Initiative, a government-funded programme, Ora Partners (a licensed trustee since 1993) built a blockchain-based trust administration platform. The Cook Islands has already shown that new technology can work inside the trustee system, led by licensed trustees.

## The offer: a registry and disclosure rails

- **Digital trust registry.** On-chain records of trust formation, transfers, trustee decisions and asset-backing attestations.
- **Disclosure and compliance rails.** Connecting existing regtech (for example Chainlink ACE, StarCompliance and Safeheron) to Cook Islands trust structures. IN$DEX does not build these tools and claims no agreements with their providers.
- **Pacific convening.** A place to bring regional regulators and trustees together and contribute to standards.

Other uses, such as private credit, funds, tourism assets and sovereign instruments, are options that depend on future legislation. They are not near-term commitments.

| CAWG scope item | What IN$DEX offers |
|---|---|
| AML/CFT compliance | Disclosure rails, designed so supervisors can see trustee decisions and transfers as they are recorded |
| Correspondent banking | Transparent asset-backing attestations that banks can check for themselves |
| Reputational risk | An auditable registry that shows the jurisdiction is proactive, not merely permissive |

CAWG is also asked to consider "alternative approaches". This is one, and it does not wait on legislation.

## Where IN$DEX sits

IN$DEX does not seek a VASP licence. It would operate inside the existing trustee perimeter, under FSC-supervised trustees, and defers to the FSC and Crown Law on what is permitted.

## The ask

A meeting in December with CAWG or the FIU to present the registry concept as a complement to their work, not a competitor to the legislation under review.
