## C. Namespace conflict check (INDEX / INDX)

Run on sns.id on 21 October 2026 through its public search box. Nothing was registered, bought or connected.

| Name searched | Result |
|---|---|
| `index.sns` | **Already registered.** Status "Listed" for sale at about 36,225 USDC. Owner shown as `C1dLe…B8LRZ` (truncated on screen). |
| `indx.sns` | **Available** (marked "Rare") at about 160.03 USDC. |

What this means and does not mean:
- SNS names end in `.sns`. SNS does not host a `.index` top-level domain, so I found no existing `.index` TLD on SNS. I could not check AllDomains' own TLD search, which is on the launch page and is part of applying.
- `index.sns` belongs to someone else, so a `.index` TLD on AllDomains could be confused with it. The two are separate namespaces, but the lookalike is a real brand and phishing risk. Decide whether that matters before applying.
- `indx.sns` is free today. Registering it, or any other name, costs money and is AJ's decision.
- The INDX token ticker is not a registry. This check covers SNS names only, not token tickers or symbols on Solana. A separate ticker check is still open.
- Doctrine today reserves `.INDX` names (`yourname.INDX`), not `.index`. Applying for `.index` would be a change of direction. Pick one before applying.
