## 1. The headline: DUNA is a Wyoming entity, not a Pacific one

The Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association was created by Wyoming SF0050, signed by Governor Mark Gordon and **effective 1 July 2024**. It is built on Wyoming's existing Unincorporated Nonprofit Association Act, adapted for DAOs. It gives a DAO legal standing to contract, hold assets, sue and be sued, and pay taxes.

**Three facts that decide this on their own:**

1. **It is a United States entity.** Adopting it gives IN$DEX a Wyoming legal home. That contradicts the live site — *"Image Nation Dex Limited is the intended Cook Islands company name"* — and it contradicts the entire Pacific-first, Cook-Islands-first positioning. It also creates a US nexus, and with it US securities, tax and sanctions exposure that a Cook Islands structure does not carry. **This is the opposite direction from where the project has said it is going, publicly.**
2. **It requires at least 100 members.** IN$DEX has **zero citizens** today. The entity is not available yet even if it were wanted.
3. **It is strictly nonprofit — no distribution of profits to members or token holders.** All surplus must go back into the mission. That needs to be checked against the 98/2 model and against anything INDX is ever intended to return to holders. ⚠️

**There is no Cook Islands DUNA equivalent.** Searched specifically; nothing found. Wyoming is the jurisdiction that did this.

## 2. The Cook Islands crypto-law picture is worse than "no framework"

This is the finding that matters most, and it cuts against the optimistic reading.

**There is no virtual-asset licensing regime in the Cook Islands.** Nothing to apply for, no VASP licence, no approved pathway. (For contrast, Turks and Caicos published a *Virtual Assets Business Bill* consultation draft in May 2026 with submissions closing 8 June 2026 — that is what a jurisdiction building a framework looks like. The Cook Islands has not done this.)

**What the Cook Islands has actually legislated on crypto is seizure, not authorisation:**

- **Tainted Cryptocurrency Recovery Bill 2023** — **withdrawn**. Crown Law considered it deeply flawed and unconstitutional and warned it could validate criminal activity. Critics described it as state-sanctioned hacking. It was drafted with input from a US firm, Drumcliffe. A special select committee recommended removing it.
- **Cryptocurrency (Ransomware Suppression) Bill 2025** — still under review, **not yet tabled** in Parliament. Copies sit with Crown Law, the Minister of Justice, the Law Society and the PM's Office.
- The **Cook Islands Chamber of Commerce** has publicly warned that crypto legislation implemented without careful consultation risks damaging banking, trade and tourism.

**Read that as a base rate.** A crypto bill in this jurisdiction took from 2023 to withdrawn, and its replacement still is not tabled three years later. That is the realistic speed of bespoke crypto lawmaking in the Cook Islands.

## 3. "Create our own with Cook Islands government approval" — the timing does not work

A new entity type means **primary legislation**: policy paper, Cabinet, Crown Law drafting, select committee, consultation, Parliament. On the evidence above, that is a multi-year path in this jurisdiction, not a multi-month one.

**And right now there is no government to approve anything.** Parliament is dissolved. The **general election is 12 August 2026 — seven days from today.** All 24 seats contested, 63 candidates, 13 needed for a majority; Cook Islands Party 20 candidates, United Party 14, Democratic Party 12. Coalition formation will take further weeks after that.

**Against the published timeline** — 30 November 2026 stability freeze, 6 December 2026 Cook Islands demonstration, 24 February 2027 public pilot — bespoke legislation is not achievable. It is not close.

**This does not mean drop the idea.** It means: do not make it a dependency of the February pilot. It is a two-to-five-year sovereignty play that can run in parallel, and it becomes far more credible once there is a working product and real citizens to point at.

## 4. Existing Cook Islands structures worth putting to an adviser

One distinction first, because it is the most common expensive mistake: the Cook Islands runs **two separate regimes**. The **domestic** regime (Companies Act 2017) is for entities actually operating in and for the Cook Islands. The **international/offshore** regime (International Companies, International LLC) is built for non-residents doing business *outside* the country. **IN$DEX serves Cook Islanders, so it belongs in the domestic regime** — and the offshore vehicles carry asset-protection-haven associations that would work directly against a financial-inclusion story with a regulator or a journalist. ⚠️

| Option | What it is | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Company limited by guarantee** | Domestic company, no share capital, members guarantee a fixed sum on winding up, surplus reinvested rather than distributed. Separate legal personality, limited liability. | Closest domestic analogue to a DUNA's nonprofit character. Worth pricing first. ⚠️ |
| **Incorporated Society** | Membership body under the Incorporated Societies Act 1994, amended 2017 (the amendment was mainly to enable the online register, not a rewrite). | Membership-based and community-legible — conceptually near MemeDAO governance. Member-count and rule requirements need checking. ⚠️ |
| **Company limited by shares** | Ordinary domestic company — the "Image Nation Dex Limited" already named on the site. | Simplest, fastest, already the stated intent. Does not by itself express the 98/2 doctrine. |
| **Hybrid** | Operating company + a guarantee company or society holding the Civilisation Fund. | Lets the 98/2 split be structural rather than a promise. Most likely to survive scrutiny. ⚠️ |

Registration is fully electronic via the Ministry of Justice registry (companies, incorporated societies and PPSR on one platform, launched December 2019 with ADB and New Zealand support, publicly searchable free, 24/7).

## 5. What I would put to an adviser, in this order

1. **Can a company limited by guarantee hold and disburse the Civilisation Fund**, with the 98/2 split written into its constitution, so the doctrine is structural rather than a promise?
2. **Does operating a non-custodial DEX and issuing INDX require any Cook Islands licence today**, given there is no VASP regime — and what is the FSC's actual posture?
3. **Does the absence of a framework create exposure** if the Ransomware Suppression Bill or a successor is passed *after* launch and applies retrospectively?
4. **Would a guarantee company or society jeopardise the ability to hold a bank account**, given the Chamber's stated banking concerns?
5. **Is "Image Nation Dex Limited" available**, and does the name imply regulated activity requiring consent?

## 6. Recommendation

**Do not adopt DUNA.** It is a US entity, unavailable below 100 members, and it points the project away from the Pacific-first position stated on the homepage.

**Do not make bespoke Cook Islands legislation a dependency of the February 2027 pilot.** Keep it as a long-term sovereignty ambition, raised properly once there is a working product, real citizens, and a seated government.

**Do incorporate under the existing domestic regime** — most likely a company limited by shares for operations, with a guarantee company or incorporated society for the Civilisation Fund — and get that in front of a Cook Islands practitioner **after the 12 August election**, when there is a government to engage with.

**Until an adviser confirms a structure, publish nothing about legal form** beyond what the site already says, which is accurate and appropriately modest: *"Image Nation Dex Limited is intended as a Cook Islands entity; registration has not yet been filed."*

**DUNA must not appear in any IN$DEX file, screen or document.** It entered via a pasted strategy document on 5 August 2026 labelled "(From Production)" — it is not on the site and never was. This is the same class as the fabricated Swiss Verein / Wyoming DAO structure purged from five files on 29 July 2026.
