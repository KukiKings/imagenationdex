Privacy, in plain language.

This website is pre-launch. There is no live citizen wallet, payment rail, remittance settlement, token distribution or issued yourname.IN$DEX domain. SIINDEX Visitor Mode is information only. Tier-0 on /tier0 is an email-preview identity test, not an account launch.

Visitor Mode (public site)

SIINDEX may keep a random visitor identifier, provider-consent choice, voice preference and a limited copy of the conversation in your browser’s local storage. Use “Clear conversation & consent” to remove that local copy.

What external providers process in Visitor Mode

  • If you use the microphone, audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription.
  • Your question or transcript may be sent to a language model provider so a reply can be generated.
  • If spoken replies are on, reply text is sent to ElevenLabs for speech.

Do not enter passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, identity documents or bank details in Visitor Mode.

Waitlist

If you apply on /waitlist.html or /join, IN$DEX may store the name, contact (email or phone), region and optional invitation code you type, so we can contact you about a founding cohort. That is not a wallet and not a token allocation.

Tier-0 email preview

If you use /tier0, Supabase Auth may send a one-time email code. After you verify, IN$DEX may store a citizen preview row: display name, email, region, optional date of birth, and a guardian email if you are under 18. Status stays preview. This does not create keys, a wallet, or a live domain.

IN$DEX does not collect a face scan, liveness selfie, MPC key shards, recovery words or payment-card data on this website.

What IN$DEX does not claim is live

Post-quantum encryption of citizen funds, zero-knowledge identity, biometric KYC, MPC / Grid wallets, regulatory viewing keys, on-chain 98/2 enforcement and seven-year AML transaction archives are not live services on this public website.

Company

Your choices

Use the site without SIINDEX. Type instead of speaking. Decline provider consent. Do not use /tier0 if you do not want an email preview row. Ask us to delete a waitlist or Tier-0 preview row by emailing the address above.
