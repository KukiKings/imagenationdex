Privacy, in plain language.

The public website does not currently provide a citizen wallet, payments, token distribution or Tier 0 account activation. This notice covers the website and SIINDEX Visitor Mode only.

What stays on your device

SIINDEX Visitor Mode keeps a random visitor identifier, provider-consent choice, voice preference and a limited copy of your conversation in your browser’s local storage. Use “Clear conversation & consent” in the SIINDEX panel to remove that local copy and reset consent.

What external providers process

  • If you choose microphone input, the audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription.
  • Your typed question or transcript and recent conversation context are sent to Anthropic so a response can be generated.
  • If spoken replies are enabled, the response text is sent to ElevenLabs to create the voice output.

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices. Do not enter passwords, seed phrases, private keys, identity documents, financial account details or other sensitive information.

What IN$DEX records

IN$DEX does not intentionally store raw microphone audio, transcripts, typed questions, generated replies or Visitor Mode conversation content on its servers. The voice services record limited security and reliability metadata such as a one-way visitor hash, time, request size, model identifier, status and error code. That metadata supports rate limiting, abuse prevention and fault diagnosis.

Website infrastructure

The website and its voice functions use hosted infrastructure. Network providers may process ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser information and request logs. The long-term sovereign and decentralised design is a roadmap direction, not a claim that every current website component is decentralised.

Waitlist and future onboarding

The approved public surface does not currently collect Tier 0 onboarding information. Before any citizen onboarding opens, IN$DEX will publish a separate notice describing the exact data, purpose, retention, deletion and legal basis. Tier 0 is designed for phone number, one-time code, name.IN$DEX selection and portal activation—no face scan.

No unverified claims

IN$DEX does not claim that post-quantum encryption, zero-knowledge identity, biometric verification, MPC wallets, regulatory viewing keys or seven-year AML retention are live on this public website.

Your choices

You can use the informational website without SIINDEX. You can type instead of using the microphone, decline provider consent, turn spoken replies off, clear the local conversation, or contact us with a privacy question.
