Glossary
FAQ
C
Civilisation Fund Governance
A shared community treasury that receives 2% of every transaction on IN$DEX. It funds infrastructure like mesh nodes, education, insurance reserves, and ambassador rewards. Citizens vote on how it is spent. Think of it like a community rates fund but run entirely by the community.
Compressed NFT Receipt Marketplace
Purchases on IN$DEX are designed to create a permanent, tamper-proof digital receipt stored on the Solana blockchain, costing a fraction of a cent to create. 🚧 Not live yet — today, purchase records are stored on IN$DEX's ledger, not on-chain.
D
DEX Wallet
Decentralised Exchange — a place to swap tokens (like INDX, SOL, USDC) directly with other people, no bank or middleman required. IN$DEX's DEX is designed to run on the Solana blockchain (Raydium CPMM), so trades would settle in under a second for less than a cent. 🚧 Not live yet — swaps aren't available today.
E
Escrow Marketplace
When you buy something on IN$DEX, your payment is held in escrow — enforced by IN$DEX's ledger system today (a Solana smart contract is planned, not live yet). The seller can't touch it until you confirm you received your item. If there's a dispute, the funds stay locked until it's resolved.
See how it works in Order Status →
G
Genesis Bonus Earn
A one-time planned gift of 1,000 INDX for every citizen who completes Sovereign Verification during the founding period. INDX has no live market price yet, so no USD value is quoted. 🚧 Not live yet — Genesis Bonuses have not been issued.
Grid Account Wallet
Your IN$DEX wallet. Unlike a regular crypto wallet, there is no recovery phrase or seed words to write down, back up, or lose — none exist, by design. Instead, your wallet is secured by Squads v4 MPC: three keys split across your device, the cloud, and a backup key. You only need 2 of the 3 to access your funds — so losing your phone doesn't mean losing your money.
Manage your Grid Account keys →
Governance Governance
Citizens with a Wisdom Score of 50 or above can vote on proposals that change how IN$DEX works — fee adjustments, new features, language support, and more. Voting power is proportional to your Wisdom Score, not how much money you have.
Go to Governance →
I
INDX Token Wallet
The currency of IN$DEX, planned as a Solana SPL Token — 100 million total supply, none minted yet. 🚧 Not live yet: today INDX exists only as a balance on IN$DEX's internal ledger, with no live market price and no USD value to quote. Once live, INDX is designed to be used to buy goods, pay for services, stake, and vote in governance.

IN$DEX Domain (.IN$DEX) Wallet
Your planned human-readable identity on the platform — like aj.IN$DEX. Instead of sharing a long wallet address (e.g. 7xKz…mN4p), anyone would be able to send you tokens just by typing your name — like an email address for your money. 🚧 Not live yet — domains are not minted today.
L
Light Node Earn
A small software program that runs on your phone or computer, helping to relay transactions across the IN$DEX Sovereign Mesh network. In return, you can earn 3–8 INDX per day for keeping your node online. You don't need special hardware — just a phone with a stable connection.
Set up a Light Node →
R
98/2 Law Marketplace
The core economic rule IN$DEX is designed around: on every transaction, 98% goes directly to the seller or creator, and 2% goes to the Civilisation Fund. There are no hidden fees, no bank cut, no platform markup. Today this is applied by IN$DEX's ledger system, not by deployed on-chain contract code — changing it is designed to require a citizen vote.
RWA (Real World Asset) Marketplace
A physical item or property designed to be tokenised — turned into a digital token on the Solana blockchain. This is meant to let you own a provable share of something real (land, art, a business) using INDX, with ownership recorded permanently on-chain. 🚧 Not live yet.
S
SIINDEX SI
Sovereign Intelligent INDEX — the Synthetic Intelligence (SI) agent swarm that acts as IN$DEX's Chief Operating Officer. She is not a chatbot — she is an autonomous agent system. 🚧 Full transaction monitoring, anomaly flagging, 24/7 citizen support, and daily operations briefings for the Wisdom Council are on the roadmap and not all live yet — the Support Agent chat is live today.

Sovereign Delivery Marketplace
A planned peer-to-peer courier network run by IN$DEX citizens. Instead of using a courier company, community members would deliver packages to each other and earn INDX for each delivery — keeping money inside the community, even in areas without traditional courier services. 🚧 Not live yet.
Sovereign Mesh Wallet
A planned decentralised internet layer designed to let IN$DEX operate even when standard internet is unavailable — in rural areas, during outages, or in regions with internet restrictions. Light Nodes would form the mesh, and offline payments would sync automatically once connectivity returns. 🚧 Not live yet — an internet connection is required today.
Sovereign Verification Governance
A tiered identity system with three levels: Citizen (phone number), Verified Citizen (government ID), and Sovereign Citizen (community vouching + biometrics). Higher tiers unlock higher transaction limits and the Genesis Bonus. Your documents are verified but never stored by IN$DEX.

Target rates: 4% (flexible), 5.5% (30 days), 7.2% (90 days), 10% (365 days) — subject to change before launch. Requires Wisdom Score 100 to unlock. The yield is designed to come from a portion of the Civilisation Fund and transaction fees.

W
Wisdom Score Governance
A reputation score (0–200) that grows as you contribute to the Republic. It starts at 20 when you join and increases as you verify your identity, buy and sell, vote on proposals, refer friends, and stake INDX. It is not based on how much money you have — it measures your contribution. Higher scores unlock features: 50 = governance, 100 = Sovereign Yield (planned, not live yet), 150 = fee reduction, 200 = Sovereign Elder.

Wisdom Council Governance
Citizens with a Wisdom Score of 150+ who form IN$DEX's governing body. They review governance proposals, mediate escalated disputes, and hold the keys to constitutional amendments. Membership is earned, not appointed.
Do I need a bank account to use IN$DEX? +
No. IN$DEX is built specifically for people without bank accounts. Once live, you'll be able to buy INDX with cash at a participating merchant, receive payments from other citizens, and buy goods — all without ever touching a bank. Cash and card funding are not live yet during the pilot; your Grid Account wallet is built to be your bank.
What happens if I lose my phone? +
Your Grid Account is secured by 3 keys (Device, Cloud, Recovery) via 2-of-3 MPC — you only need 2 of 3 to access your funds. If you lose your phone, your Cloud Key (in iCloud or Google Drive) and Recovery Key are enough to restore full access from a new device. There are no recovery words to lose — none exist for this wallet, ever. See security settings →
Is my money safe when I buy something? +
Yes — your payment is protected by escrow. Today, escrow is enforced by IN$DEX's ledger system (a live Solana smart contract is planned, not live yet). The seller cannot access the funds until you release them, or until 7 days pass with no dispute. If something goes wrong, you can open a dispute and the funds stay locked until it's resolved.
How do I earn INDX without buying it? +
Several ways: sell goods on the marketplace (+INDX per sale), run a Light Node (3–8 INDX/day for staying online), refer friends (+10 Wisdom Score, bonus INDX), complete verification (Genesis Bonus of 1,000 INDX — planned, not live yet), or stake INDX to target 4–10% yield (planned, not live yet). Sovereign Delivery is also planned, not live yet.
What is Wisdom Score and why does it matter? +
Wisdom Score is your reputation on IN$DEX. It starts at 20 when you join and grows with every contribution — buying, selling, verifying, voting, referring. It is not about wealth. A citizen with 10 INDX and WS 80 has more governance power than one with 10,000 INDX and WS 30. Key milestones: WS 50 = vote on proposals, WS 100 = unlock Sovereign Yield (planned, not live yet), WS 150 = fee reduction + Wisdom Council eligibility.
Why does IN$DEX use Solana? +
Solana processes transactions in under a second for less than $0.01. This makes it practical for everyday commerce — unlike Ethereum, where fees can exceed the value of small purchases. IN$DEX is built on Solana's SPL Token standard and plans to use Metaplex Bubblegum for compressed NFT receipts (design cost ~$0.000005 to mint). 🚧 On-chain settlement and receipts are not live yet.
How does Sovereign Delivery work? +
🚧 Not live yet. The design: when a seller selects "Sovereign Delivery," they'd mark the package ready for collection, IN$DEX would notify nearby citizen couriers who could claim it, and the courier would earn INDX on delivery confirmation — no courier company needed, the community delivers to itself.
What is the Civilisation Fund and who controls it? +
The Civilisation Fund receives 2% of every transaction on IN$DEX. It is controlled collectively by all citizens through governance votes. Currently it funds mesh node infrastructure, sovereign education, the insurance reserve, and ambassador rewards. No individual controls it — decisions require a majority citizen vote.
Can I convert INDX back to USD or local currency? +
Not yet — this is the target design, not live today. Once live, you'll be able to swap INDX for USDC (a stablecoin pegged to the US Dollar) via the DEX Swap screen, then withdraw USDC to a bank or exchange. Tier 1 KYC verification will be required for fiat withdrawals above $3,200/day. 🚧 Not live yet. Go to DEX Swap →
What if I have a problem with a purchase? +
You have 7 days after dispatch to open a dispute. Go to Order Status → Open Dispute. Your escrow funds stay locked while the dispute is active. 🚧 Not live yet: automatic SIINDEX resolution for simple disputes and the Sovereign Court jury system for complex cases are planned — during the pilot, disputes are handled by IN$DEX support. See Buyer Protection →
Is IN$DEX available offline? +
Not yet — Sovereign Mesh (peer-to-peer offline payments over Bluetooth/WiFi-Direct, syncing to Solana once reconnected) is planned but not live today. Right now IN$DEX requires an internet connection. 🚧 Not live yet. See Offline Mode →
