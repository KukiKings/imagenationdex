Current limitations
Answers come from a fixed, rule-based briefing written into this page — no live database query and no live language-model call in this build.
A more capable Executive Mode backend (siindex-runtime, with live founder dashboard data) already exists on the Supabase project, found during this build. It requires a founder login + AAL2 MFA step-up flow that isn't built yet, so this preview doesn't call it — that's the natural next milestone.
Speech-to-text is your browser's built-in engine. Safari has little or no support for it — use Type Instead there.
The spoken voice is your browser's default system voice, not SIINDEX's final ElevenLabs voice.
This page has no login and no access control. Keep it local and unlinked until real founder authentication is added.
Privacy notice
This preview runs entirely in your browser. If you use the microphone, Chrome sends your audio to its own built-in speech-recognition service to produce the transcript (standard Chrome behaviour, not an IN$DEX or ElevenLabs service in this build). Spoken replies use your browser's own voice engine. Nothing you say or type is sent to any IN$DEX server, stored anywhere, or used to train anything. Closing this tab clears everything.
