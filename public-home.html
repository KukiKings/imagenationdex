Where this started

A coconut, a roadside sale, and no change.

The founder saw a Cook Islands seller lose an ordinary sale because a visitor had no small cash and there was no simple digital alternative. IN$DEX began with that practical question: what would work for her, on her phone, without requiring her to become a technology expert?

“If Mama Noe cannot understand it and use it, it is not finished.”

The Mama Noe Test is the citizen-facing standard: plain words, clear choices, voice and typed access, no hidden promise, and no feature labelled live before a real person can complete it.
