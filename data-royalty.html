💸 How royalties are designed to work
When companies purchase IN$DEX citizen data from third parties, they'd pay into the Data Royalty Pool, and SIINDEX would automatically distribute 98% to the citizens whose data was sold — 2% to the Civilisation Fund, same as every other payout on IN$DEX. That detection and payment pipeline doesn't exist yet, so nothing is happening automatically today.

Royalty Pool FAQ
What counts as a "data sale"?▾
Once live, any confirmed transfer of your data from a third party to a buyer that SIINDEX can verify — starting with behavioural, location, financial, health and demographic categories.
How is the split decided?▾
98% to the citizen whose data was sold, 2% to the Civilisation Fund — the same split used across every other payout on IN$DEX.
When does this go live?▾
No launch date has been set. Turn on "Notify me at launch" above and SIINDEX will let you know the moment detection and payouts go live.
Is the calculator above real?▾
No — it's a hypothetical illustration of the 98/2 split at today's INDX price. No real sale has happened.
