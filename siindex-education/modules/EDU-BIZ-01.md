# EDU-BIZ-01 — What is value?

**Track:** Business basics  
**Status:** Planned (draft)  
**Language of public UI:** English

## Goal
Name one product or service you can offer that someone will pay for.

## Why it matters
Unbanked and Pacific citizens already create value (food, craft, care, labour). Naming it is the first step to ownership and fair trade — Mama Noe test.

## Steps
1. List three things you already know how to do.  
2. Circle one a neighbour would pay for.  
3. Write the offer in one plain sentence.  
4. Say the price idea out loud (USD display rules in product UI).  

## Check
Can Mama Noe understand the offer in one read?
