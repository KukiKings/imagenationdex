# EDU-FIN-04 — Live vs Planned labels

**Track:** Digital finance · **Status:** Planned (draft)

## Goal
Read any IN$DEX status label without confusion.

## Steps
1. Live = usable today.  
2. Testing / Planned / Paused = not full public product.  
3. Never assume wallets or payments are live.
