# EDU-LANG-01 — Why language is wealth

**Track:** Language  
**Status:** Planned (draft)  

## Goal
Explain why preserving Pacific / Cook Islands language is part of sovereign wealth.

## Why it matters
Language carries law, story, place, and trade. Digital civilisation must not erase it.

## Steps
1. Name your language(s) and one place name that matters.  
2. Write one greeting and one thank-you.  
3. Link language to identity (`yourname.IN$DEX` as a name, not only a wallet string).  

## Check
Would an elder recognise respect in this lesson?
