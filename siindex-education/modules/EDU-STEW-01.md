# EDU-STEW-01 — Ocean as livelihood

**Track:** Ocean / land stewardship  
**Status:** Planned (draft)  

## Goal
Describe the ocean as livelihood and responsibility, not only extract.

## Why it matters
Pacific economies and identity depend on healthy ocean and honest practice.

## Steps
1. Name one ocean resource your community relies on.  
2. Name one risk (overuse, waste, false claims).  
3. Write one care action you can take this month.  

## Check
Does the lesson avoid promising mineral riches or invented rights?
