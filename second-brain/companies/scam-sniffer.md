# Scam Sniffer

**Type:** Web3 anti-phishing / wallet-drainer analytics.
**Relationship to IN$DEX:** Data source only. Not engaged, not evaluated as a vendor.

**Why it's in the record (2026-07-30):**
Supplies the loss baseline in `pqsi-hardening-research-2026-07-30.md` §1.1 — wallet-drainer phishing losses **$83.85M in 2025, down 83%** from ~$494M in 2024; **106,000 victims, down 68%**; **average loss $790**; only 11 incidents above $1M (down from 30). Reported via Cointelegraph/Cryptonews rather than read from Scam Sniffer directly.

**Two caveats recorded with the figures, and they must travel with them:**
- The decline is partly a **bear-market artefact** — losses track token prices. Do not present the 83% fall as evidence the ecosystem got safer.
- **Permit-signature attacks were 38% of incidents above $1M** — large losses are dominated by signature-based, not balance-based, techniques.

**Why the average matters here:** $790 is roughly a month's income in parts of the Pacific. This is the sourced basis for PQSI Addition 7 — **size risk to the citizen's net worth, not to absolute dollars.** No mainstream wallet does this; it is cheap and it is a genuine differentiator.

**Status:** Source of record.
