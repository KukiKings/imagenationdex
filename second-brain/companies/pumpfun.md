# Pump.fun

**Type:** Solana meme-coin bonding-curve launchpad
**Relevance to IN$DEX:** Evaluated and **rejected** as a launch venue for INDX (Session 122, 2026-07-29, `launchpad-research-2026.md`).

**Why it is architecturally impossible for INDX**
Every bonding-curve launchpad — Pump.fun, Meteora DBC, Raydium LaunchLab — *mints its own token* as part of the curve. INDX already exists on Solana mainnet with mint and freeze authority burned, so no launchpad can list it. Meteora DBC states this outright. The finding closed the launchpad question by architecture rather than preference: **Raydium CPMM is the only venue able to list INDX**, confirming whitepaper Section 11.6 was already correct.

**The declined "test launch"**
A pasted proposal suggested launching a throwaway token on Pump.fun (~$102), marketing it to the IN$DEX community, and using the profits to fund the real launch — while itself citing a **0.198% graduation rate**. Declined on three grounds: technically impossible for INDX; it asks the project's earliest believers (canonically, the unbanked) to fund the founder through a near-certain loss; and it contradicts the 98/2 doctrine and the standing ban on FOMO and rug-pull mechanics. Recorded as something an agent should not execute on a founder's behalf.

**Context note:** LaunchLab overtook Pump.fun in volume Jul–Aug 2025. Prior canon already classed Pump.fun as "meme coin infrastructure, no customization — INDX is not a meme."

**Status:** Closed. No further evaluation needed unless INDX's mint architecture changes (it will not — authorities are burned).
