# BTIB — Business Trade and Investment Board (Cook Islands)

**Type:** Cook Islands government agency, btib.gov.ck
**Relevance to IN$DEX:** approves any **foreign-owned** business operating in the Cook Islands, separately from and in addition to anything the [[cook-islands-fsc|FSC]] does.

**Verified (desk research, 19 Jul 2026, re-cited 5 Aug 2026):**
- Foreign-investment approval required for any foreign-owned business.
- Minimum capital injection **NZD $1,000,000** (Rarotonga) / **NZD $500,000** (outer islands).
- Application fee NZD $750 standard (~30 working days) or NZD $850 express (~5 working days).
- BTIB states company names must be in English; the earlier claim that the registry rejects `$` in a name was **inferred, not verified** (correction of record, 30 Jul).

**Why this probably does not apply to IN$DEX:** AJ holds Cook Islands / New Zealand citizenship (confirmed by AJ, 19 Jul 2026). On the citizen pathway the BTIB foreign-investment step, the NZD $500K–$1M capital injection and the NZD $750 fee **do not apply** — registration is NZD $75 via the Ministry of Justice registry. The foreign-investor figures are retained here as background, not as IN$DEX's situation. ⚠️ Not confirmed by a Cook Islands practitioner.
