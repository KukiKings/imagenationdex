# FSC — Financial Supervisory Commission (Cook Islands)

**Type:** Cook Islands financial regulator, fsc.gov.ck
**Relevance to IN$DEX:** the licensing authority for financial services in the jurisdiction IN$DEX intends to incorporate in. Co-chairs the **Cryptocurrency Advisory Working Group (CAWG)** through its Financial Intelligence Unit, alongside the Office of the Prime Minister.

**Verified:**
- Licenses remittance businesses under the **Money Changing & Remittance Businesses Act 2009**. Requirements: local office, local staff, operations actually performed in the Cook Islands, an appointed auditor before lodging, and a **face-to-face meeting in the Cook Islands with both FSC and [[btib|BTIB]]**. No statutory minimum capital; industry guidance suggests not less than **USD $100,000**. Annual renewal 1 April.
- **There is no virtual-asset / VASP licensing regime in the Cook Islands.** Nothing to apply for. Re-confirmed 5 Aug 2026.
- What the jurisdiction has legislated on crypto is **seizure, not authorisation**: the Tainted Cryptocurrency Recovery Bill 2023 was **withdrawn** (Crown Law considered it unconstitutional); the Cryptocurrency (Ransomware Suppression) Bill 2025 is still under review and **not yet tabled**.
- ⏳ **No government to engage right now.** Parliament dissolved 25 Jun 2026; general election **12 Aug 2026** (one week out as of this note). Coalition formation adds weeks. FSC review expected December per the companies index.

**Open (⏳, for a licensed Cook Islands adviser, not settled here):** whether operating a non-custodial DEX and issuing INDX requires any Cook Islands licence today given there is no VASP regime; whether a bill passed *after* launch could apply retrospectively.
