## The finding: DUNA is not available and points the wrong way

The **Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association** is a **Wyoming** entity (SF0050, effective 1 Jul 2024). Three facts decide it on their own:

1. **It is a US entity.** Adopting it gives IN$DEX a Wyoming legal home, contradicting the live site (*"Image Nation Dex Limited is the intended Cook Islands company name"*) and the entire Pacific-first position — and creating US securities, tax and sanctions nexus a Cook Islands structure does not carry.
2. **It requires at least 100 members.** IN$DEX has **zero citizens** today. Not available even if wanted.
3. **Strictly nonprofit** — no distribution to members or token holders. ⚠️ Needs checking against the 98/2 model and anything INDX is ever meant to return.

**There is no Cook Islands DUNA equivalent.** Searched specifically; Wyoming is the jurisdiction that did this.

**Standing instruction: DUNA must not appear in any IN$DEX file, screen or document.** It entered via a pasted strategy document labelled "(From Production)" on 5 Aug 2026. It is not on the site and never was. Same class as the fabricated **Swiss Verein / Wyoming DAO LLC** structure purged from five files on 29 Jul 2026 — a legal form arriving from a pasted document rather than from a decision.

## "Create our own, Cook Islands government approved" — the timing does not work

A new entity type means primary legislation: policy paper, Cabinet, Crown Law drafting, select committee, consultation, Parliament. The base rate in this jurisdiction: a crypto bill went from 2023 to **withdrawn**, and its replacement is still not tabled three years later. And Parliament is dissolved — election **12 Aug 2026**, coalition formation after that.

Against 30 Nov 2026 (stability freeze), 6 Dec 2026 (Cook Islands demonstration) and 24 Feb 2027 (public pilot), bespoke legislation is **not achievable**. Keep it as a two-to-five-year sovereignty play; **do not make it a dependency of the February pilot**.

## The distinction that costs money if missed

The Cook Islands runs **two separate regimes**. **Domestic** (Companies Act 2017) is for entities operating in and for the Cook Islands. **International/offshore** (International Company, International LLC) is for non-residents doing business *outside* the country. **IN$DEX serves Cook Islanders, so it belongs in the domestic regime** — and offshore vehicles carry asset-protection-haven associations that work directly against a financial-inclusion story in front of a regulator or a journalist. ⚠️

| Option | Fit |
|---|---|
| Company limited by guarantee | Closest domestic analogue to a DUNA's nonprofit character. Price first. ⚠️ |
| Incorporated Society (Act 1994, am. 2017) | Membership-based, community-legible, near Citizen Governance conceptually. Member-count rules need checking. ⚠️ |
| Company limited by shares | Simplest, already the stated intent (`Image Nation DEX Limited`). Does not express 98/2 by itself. |
| **Hybrid** — operating company + guarantee company/society holding the Civilisation Fund | Makes 98/2 **structural rather than a promise**. Most likely to survive scrutiny. ⚠️ |
